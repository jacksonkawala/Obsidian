## Concepts

- [Freeboard](wiki/concepts/freeboard.md) — Vertical distance between pool surface and dam crest. Critical TSF safety parameter with TARP-level monitoring. Links to CSSM through pore pressure and effective stress.
- [Critical State Soil Mechanics](wiki/concepts/critical-state-soil-mechanics.md) — Cross-domain concept page tying CSSM properties, state, behaviour, CPT interpretation, NorSand, and tailings liquefaction practice together.
- [State Parameter](wiki/concepts/state-parameter.md) — $\psi = e - e_c$; central state variable for liquefaction susceptibility, CPT interpretation, and characteristic-state design thresholds.
- [Critical State Line](wiki/concepts/critical-state-line.md) — CSL definition, semi-log forms, measurement cautions, and relationship to state-parameter calculations.
- [Effective Stress](wiki/concepts/effective-stress.md) — Soil skeleton stress concept linking pore pressure, water management, freeboard, and liquefaction risk.
- [NorSand](wiki/concepts/norsand.md) — State-parameter-based constitutive model used for liquefaction computation, CPT inversion, and finite-element analysis.
- [Critical Friction Ratio](wiki/concepts/critical-friction-ratio.md) — Critical-state stress ratio $M$, reference value $M_{tc}$, and distinction from density-dependent peak strength.
- [Nerlerk](wiki/concepts/nerlerk.md) — Hydraulic-fill static liquefaction case history linking CPT interpretation, stress-level bias, and loose-zone variability.
- [Molikpaq](wiki/concepts/molikpaq.md) — Beaufort Sea high-cycle loading case history for cyclic pore-pressure generation and cyclic mobility.
- [Oosterschelde](wiki/concepts/oosterschelde.md) — Rowe and Craig centrifuge-test context showing the disproportionate effect of loose pockets.
- [GISTM](wiki/concepts/gistm.md) — Tailings-management governance concept linked to defensible CSSM-based liquefaction assessment.
