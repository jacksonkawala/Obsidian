# GISTM

GISTM is the Global Industry Standard on Tailings Management. In this wiki, it is relevant as the governance frame around tailings facility safety, risk identification, and defensible technical assessment.

CSSM does not replace GISTM governance. It supports it by giving a mechanics-based method for evaluating liquefaction susceptibility, pore-pressure sensitivity, and stability of tailings facilities.

## CSSM Relevance

- [[State Parameter]] profiles can make liquefaction susceptibility explicit.
- [[Critical State Soil Mechanics]] provides a defensible link between laboratory testing, CPT interpretation, and design assumptions.
- [[NorSand]] or similar models can support analyses where simple limit-equilibrium methods cannot represent strain softening.
