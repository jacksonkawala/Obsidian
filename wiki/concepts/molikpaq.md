# Molikpaq

Molikpaq was a caisson-type offshore drilling platform in the Canadian Beaufort Sea. Jefferies and Been use it as a high-cycle loading case history: ice loading generated cyclic pore pressures in the sand core and caused settlement, even though the fill was not in a highly loose state.

## CSSM Lessons

- Cyclic pore-pressure generation can affect lightly dilatant sands as well as loose sands.
- Static bias, principal stress rotation, and drainage conditions matter to cyclic mobility.
- Field instrumentation from Molikpaq helped validate laboratory and constitutive interpretations of cyclic loading.
