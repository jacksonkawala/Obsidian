# Oosterschelde

Oosterschelde refers here to the Dutch delta works context used in the Rowe and Craig centrifuge tests discussed in Chapter 5 of Jefferies and Been. The tests modelled storm-surge caissons founded on sandy seabeds and examined how discrete loose pockets changed cyclic loading performance.

## CSSM Lessons

- Small volumes of loose material can disproportionately control pore-pressure generation.
- Loose pockets can spread excess pore pressure into denser surrounding sand.
- Variability and connected weak zones matter more than average density for liquefaction assessment.

This case supports using a characteristic [[State Parameter]] value, typically toward the loose tail of the distribution, rather than relying on the mean state.
