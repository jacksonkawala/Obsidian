# Daily Geotechnical Report — 17 April 2026

**Ref:** 087/GTC/2026 | **Date:** 17-04-2026 | **Time:** 06:00–11:00

## Highlights

### WCPC Water Body — RED ALERT (seventh consecutive day, slight further deterioration)
Water level at 9.95 mRL, freeboard **1.05 m** — down 0.05 m from 1.10 m on 16 Apr (itself down from 1.15 m held for five straight). Condition remains critical and non-compliant. Godwin pump to continue running; all inflows to remain suspended.

### MSP Rejects — Paddock 16C Backflow Still Worsening
The backflow and sidewall erosion in Paddock 16C is **still progressing** — now three days after MSP Rejects were redirected to P16C (15 Apr) and two days after the backflow was first reported (16 Apr). Discharge to be reallocated and extended without further delay. Contrast the 16C berm freeboards — 2.50 m (P16C sidewall) and 2.80 m (starboard) — which are geometrically adequate. The issue is redirected-flow dynamics in an already-accumulated paddock, not overtopping.

### BB1 Stacker Return Water Migrating to Starboard Sidewall — Fresh Concern
Field observations indicate BB1 stacker return water is migrating towards the starboard sidewall, causing progressive erosion, compromising berm structural integrity, and increasing the likelihood of a potential breach. Agreed response: redirect BB1 discharge to face the mining pond and reinstate the affected sidewall section.

### Full-Scope Inspection Restored
Unlike the 16 Apr report (which omitted routine coverage of WCPB, WCPC in detail, SMO, Paddock 9C/16C, Old DM Pit, Exit Pond, Starboard Side Corner, and the eastern paddocks), the 17 Apr report resumes full-site inspection — 44 pages including a priority-actions table for the week.
