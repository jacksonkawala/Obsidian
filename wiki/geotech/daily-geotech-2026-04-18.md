## Highlights

Paddock 73 WL rose from 41.60 mRL to **41.85 mRL in 24 hours**, exceeding the maximum allowable limit by 150 mm and triggering an **Orange Alert**. Berm 73 freeboard dropped to **1.85 m (Orange)**. Seepage continues to daylight at the toe of Berm 73. Dewatering initiated by starting up the pump; outflows now include a Godwin and a Hippo pump to PDP-1.

### WCPC Water Body — Still Red Alert (Day 8, Freeboard Unchanged)

### WCPC Concentrator — New Beaching Risk

Oversize material accumulating behind the WCPC concentrator has formed an **island**, raising the risk of the plant beaching. Recommendation: move the concentrator forward to deeper water. Related: C1 tails O/E discharge accumulation in the dredge pond is also increasing — marked "Safe to Operate = No" for the first time in this series.

### MSP Rejects — Paddock 16C Backflow Still Worsening

The backflow and sidewall erosion first reported on 16 Apr are **still progressing** — now four days after MSP Rejects were redirected to P16C. Discharge to be reallocated and extended without further delay.

### HTC Buildup — Backflow Risk Toward Mualadi Lake

The Hot Tails O/E buildup behind WCPB exceeds the 1.0 m freeboard from beach to NGL, raising the risk of backflow toward Mualadi Lake. Recommendation: maintain ≥1.0 m minimum freeboard, optimise HTC operating hours, extend/reallocate HTC discharge.
