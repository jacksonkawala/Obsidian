## Highlights

### Old DM Pit — New Orange Alert (+0.15 m in 24 h)

WL rose from 21.00 mRL to **21.15 mRL**, exceeding the maximum allowable limit by 150 mm and triggering an **Orange Alert**. Berm 12C freeboard now **1.85 m (Yellow, flagged Orange)**. ### WCPC Water Body — Still Red Alert (Day 10+, Slight WL Improvement)

WL dropped to 9.80 mRL (from 9.95 on 18 Apr), lifting freeboard to **1.20 m (still Red)**. That is better than the 1.05 m held on 17–18 Apr but remains non-compliant. Counting from the first Red day on 11 Apr, this is **at least day 10** of the WCPC Red Alert. Godwin pump continues; inflows recommended halted (though HMC stacker still listed as an active inflow — see [[#Data Quality Notes]]).

### Tails 3 Stacker — Retention Berm Overtopped

Tailings from the Tails 3 stacker on the TSF stockpile **overtopped the retention berm** at the relocated position — a clear escalation beyond the fresh washout of 16 Apr and the "narrow and short" retention-berm risk flagged on 17 Apr. Risks called out: HME bog-down, dissipator blockage, Lift 1 sidewall erosion, access-road flooding. Corrective actions in progress: stacker stopped and relocated (done), retention berms to be built, density management (plant on shut), remediation in progress, spotter-assisted access, safe-distance rules.

### WCPC Concentrator — Beaching Risk Persists

The oversize-material island behind the WCPC concentrator (first flagged 18 Apr) remains a beaching risk. Recommendation: move the plant forward to deeper water. C1 tails O/E displacement continues to encroach.

### HTC — Backflow Risk Toward Mualadi Lake

Hot Tails O/E buildup continues to exceed the 1.0 m freeboard from beach to NGL, sustaining the backflow risk toward Mualadi Lake (flagged 18 Apr).

3. **TSF one-day rise of +0.45 m (24.30 → 24.75 mRL) not called out as an alert**, whereas Old DM Pit's +0.15 m (21.00 → 21.15 mRL) triggered an Orange Alert narrative. The asymmetry likely reflects that TSF freeboard (5.25 m) is still above Yellow whereas Old DM Pit exceeded a hard maximum-allowable-WL limit — but the contrast is worth flagging.
4. **Copy-paste text recurring**: "Erosion on the 110 kV pole – reinstatement required" appears under both WCP-A Dredge Pond and PDP-1; the HTC paragraph appears on both p.2 (under WCP-A) and p.25 (under HTC). The "just reinstated liner … ripped off" paragraph appears twice (PDP-2 and PDP-1) as on 18 Apr.
5. **Tails 3 stacker narrative is duplicated** — identical text on p.1 (Highlights) and p.5 (Tails 3A section).
6. **"Managers informed" colour shading varies** (yellow for P73, P9C, WCPC Dredge Pond, ISOA; red for WCPC Water Body and WCPB Corner) with no legend explaining the code.
7. **WCPC Water Body inflows still list HMC stacker** despite the remedial recommendation to halt all inflows — the recommendation has not been acted on.
8. **WCPC Dredge Pond freeboards both exactly 1.70 m** with "Waiting for survey update" — values provisional pending survey.
9. **BB1/Kwale, BB1 variance columns unsigned** — 30.00 − 28.80 = 1.20 is shown as "1.20" without a sign. Reader must infer direction.
10. **WCPA water balance (in the TSF summary for the same day, for context) shows +44,927 m³/d** — large positive imbalance suggesting missing outflows or double-counted inflows (not a geotech-report issue but relevant to the site-wide picture).
11. **Paddock 9C "WL to be confirmed"** with the same 17.00 mRL for six straight readings — likely stale.
12. **Many other facilities show previous-day = today across the 18→20 Apr gap.** Plausible for stable ponds, suspicious for active ones. Trend charts, however, do show 7 data points ending 20-Apr, implying daily readings were taken on 19 Apr even though no 19 Apr PDF was issued.
13. **18 Apr geotech had Paddock 73 at 41.85 mRL (Orange); today 40.90 mRL with "change = 0.00"** — the ~0.95 m drawdown happened within the reporting gap and isn't captured anywhere in the 20 Apr report as a trend metric. Mildly misleading if this report is read without the 18 Apr report at hand.
