# Daily Geotechnical Report — 22 April 2026

**Ref:** 091/GTC/2026 | **Date:** 22-04-2026 | **Time:** 06:00–11:00 | **Scope:** full-site (~44 pages)

## Highlights

### Old DMB Pit — Red Alert Continues

Old DMB Pit remains at **21.50 mRL** with **1.50 m freeboard (Red Alert)**. No inflows are reporting to the pit, but the separation berm between Old DM Pit and Paddock 11C continues to erode and still requires reinstatement.

### WCPC Water Body — Orange but Still Critical

WCPC Water Body remains at **9.60 mRL** with **1.40 m freeboard (Orange Alert)**. The report still calls the condition critical and recommends continuing the Godwin pump, raising low sections, and suspending all inflows.

### WCPB HTC Line — New Backflow Risk Toward Lake Johari

The WCPB HTC line is discharging on the starboard side of the pond and has built up significantly, reducing the minimum 1.00 m freeboard between tails and sidewall. The report flags backflow risk toward **Lake Johari** and agrees to reposition the discharge to a higher-freeboard area.

### SMO Berm Mining Risk

HME is partially mining the SMO Pond berm, reducing freeboard and weakening the protective barrier. The report recommends stopping mining toward the pond, reinstating the berm, and barricading the pond side, especially for night-shift visibility.

### MSP Rejects and Tails 3A Are Still Active Risks

MSP Rejects in Paddock 16C are building up to berm elevation, with backflow/overtopping risk. Tails 3A on the TSF stockpile is still overtopping the narrow/short retention berm, with HME bogging, dissipator blockage, and Lift 1 delay risks.
