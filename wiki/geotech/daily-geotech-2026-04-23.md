# Daily Geotechnical Report — 23 April 2026

**Ref:** 092/GTC/2026 | **Date:** 23-04-2026 | **Time:** 06:00–11:00 | **Scope:** full-site (~44 pages)

## Highlights

### Tails 3A Retention Berm Overtopping Persists

Tails 3A on the TSF stockpile remains the dominant unresolved construction risk. The report again says tailings overtopped the narrow/short retention berm, with liquefied-tailings HME bogging risk, dissipator blockage risk, and Lift 1 delay risk.

### WCPB HTC Line Still Risks Backflow Toward Lake Johari

The HTC discharge on the WCPB starboard side continues to build up and reduce the 1.00 m tailings-to-sidewall freeboard. Discharge repositioning remains the agreed mitigation.

### WCPC Water Body Holds at Orange, Still Critical

WCPC Water Body remains at **9.60 mRL**, **1.40 m freeboard (Orange Alert)**. The condition is still called critical; the Godwin pump is to continue, low sections are to be raised, and inflows suspended.

### MSP Rejects Continue to Stress Paddock 16C

MSP Rejects in Paddock 16C are still building up toward berm elevation, with backflow, berm erosion, and overtopping risk. Recommendation: close monitoring and regular relocation of the discharge point.
