# Daily Geotechnical Report — 24 April 2026

**Ref:** 093/GTC/2026 | **Date:** 24-04-2026 | **Time:** 06:00–11:00 | **Scope:** full-site (~44 pages)

## Highlights

### WCPC Pond — Plant Beached, Water Raised to Refloat Level

WCPC plant remains **beached on sand**. The WCP-C Dredge Pond water level has been raised to the agreed **10.50 mRL** refloat level so the plant can move into deeper water. The report states the plant is not operational and water should not exceed 10.50 mRL; once relocated, the pond is to be dewatered back to compliant levels.

### WCPC Water Body — Orange, Still Critical

WCPC Water Body remains at **9.60 mRL**, **1.40 m freeboard (Orange Alert)**. The critical-condition wording continues, with the same recommendations: Godwin pumping, raising low sections, and suspending inflows.

### Tails 3A Overtopping Continues

Tails 3A is still overtopping the retention berm on the TSF stockpile. The same risks remain live: HME bogging in liquefied tailings, equipment damage, operational downtime, dissipator blockage, and Lift 1 construction delays.

### WCPB HTC Backflow Risk Continues

HTC discharge on the WCPB starboard side continues to build up and reduce the minimum 1.00 m freeboard between tails and sidewall, sustaining backflow risk toward Lake Johari. The agreed response is to extend or reposition discharge to a higher-freeboard side.
