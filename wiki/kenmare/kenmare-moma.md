# Kenmare Moma Mining (Mauritius) Limited

Heavy mineral sands mining operation at Moma, Mozambique. Also operates as Kenmare Moma Processing (Mauritius) Limited.

## Latest Operational Update (24 Apr 2026)

- **[[Nataka TSF]]** — 63% filled by 23 Apr; 24 Apr geotech shows WL 24.70 mRL and **5.30 m freeboard (Yellow)**. Tails 3A still overtopping and eroding Lift 1 sidewall; Lift 1 remains 76% complete.
- **WCP-C Dredge Pond / WCPC plant** — plant is **beached and not operational**. Pond raised to **10.50 mRL** on 24 Apr to refloat the plant; water should not exceed 10.50 mRL and must be dewatered after relocation.
- **WCPC Water Body** — still **Orange but critical** at 9.60 mRL and 1.40 m freeboard through 24 Apr. Godwin pumping and inflow suspension remain recommended.
- **Old DMB Pit** — Red Alert persisted at 21.50 mRL / 1.50 m on 21-23 Apr, then improved to 21.35 mRL / 1.65 m on 24 Apr but is still called Red. Paddock 11C separation-berm erosion continues.
- **WCPB HTC line** — 22-24 Apr reports add a new backflow thread: HTC discharge on the WCPB starboard side is reducing minimum freeboard and posing backflow risk toward **Lake Johari**.
- **SMO / MSP Rejects** — SMO berm mining by HME was flagged on 22 Apr; MSP Rejects in Paddock 16C continue building toward berm elevation with backflow/overtopping risk.
- **Persistent data-quality issues** — Paddock 9C remains stuck at 17.00 mRL, PDP-1 fields go blank on 23-24 Apr, Tails 1B remains marked Safe despite major variances, and several tables carry blank or contradictory values.

- **Tails 3 stacker retention berm OVERTOPPED on 20 Apr** — escalation beyond the 16 Apr fresh washout and 17 Apr "narrow and short" retention-berm concern. Plant on shut; remediation in progress.
- **WCPC Concentrator beaching risk (new 18 Apr, still open 21 Apr)** — oversize-material island behind the plant; move concentrator to deeper water. **C1 tails O/E flagged "Safe to Operate = No"** on 18, 20, 21 Apr (three days).
- **SMO separation-berm breach risk** — escalated 17 Apr, front-berm FB dipped to 0.90 m on 18 Apr, steadily recovering: 1.20 m (20 Apr) → **1.30 m (21 Apr)**. Tailings encroaching with slumps persist.
- **Old DMB / Paddock 11C separation-berm progressive erosion** — persistent 17, 18, 20, 21 Apr; reinstatement required. Now compounded by Old DMB Pit Red Alert.
- **WCPB Starboardside Corner Red Alert** — WL **unchanged at 14.50 mRL for five readings** (17, 18, 20, 21 Apr — plus prior). River-sidewall freeboard 1.50 m; dewatering in progress; reshape plans considered.
- **BB1 return-water migration concern** — 17 Apr narrative not repeated in standalone form on 18, 20, 21 Apr, but on 21 Apr embedded in BB1 / BB1_Kwale remarks ("construct retention berm to prevent water/slurry migration towards DMC pit"). Still unresolved.
- **HTC backflow risk toward Mualadi Lake** — flagged 18 and 20 Apr (buildup exceeds 1.0 m freeboard from beach to NGL). Not explicitly in Highlights on 21 Apr but referenced under WCPB water bodies.
- **WCPA Pond** — WL 38.05 mRL on 21 Apr (−0.05). 110 kV pole erosion outstanding across four consecutive reports.
- **Liner theft — relentless.** Fresh starter-wall liner thefts recorded on 17, 18, 20, 21 Apr; PDP-1 and PDP-2 port-sidewall liners repeatedly ripped off (17, 18, 20, 21 Apr — PDP-1 and PDP-2 paragraphs verbatim identical on all four reports). Note that Weeks 16 and 17 both had zero rainfall, so exposed-liner erosion is still driven by earlier April rain (MTD 243 mm, YTD 801 mm).
- **Rainfall-induced erosion (residual)** — access roads, berms, Habelo Valley separation berm, Valley 3 embankment, Pilivili Top Embankment, River Diversion Channel, Float-off Pond entrance, DMC Top embankment.
- **Pipeline integrity** — PDP-2 starboard erosion adjacent to Berm 75 **explicitly worsening** 18, 20, 21 Apr. Pipeline leaks from PDP-1/Berm 75 pose dual risk. Slimes pipeline at TSF starter-wall bend hanging into the facility — progressive collapse risk (unchanged 17–21 Apr).
- **TSF pool trajectory volatile** — 24.60 (16 Apr) → 24.10 (17 Apr, 0.5 m drawdown) → 24.75 (19 Apr) → 24.70 (20 Apr) → 24.70 (21 Apr, with narrative contradicting at 24.75). Freeboard **5.30 m — 0.30 m from Orange**. Four pumps commissioned; return ratio varies 31–217% of daily slurry.
- **TSF 61% filled** (728,620 t on 20 Apr; reassessed up from 59% on 16 Apr via production-forecast updates). 91 days to Phase 1 capacity (20 Jul 2026). Bathymetric survey still urgently needed to verify the unverified 1.2 Mt capacity.
- **Lift 1 construction resumed 19 Apr** — 600 m³ overnight after five-day stall, 1,370 m³ on 20 Apr; completion slipped to 29 May 2026, margin 52 days.
- **Paddock 9C** — seven consecutive readings at 17.00 mRL with "WL to be confirmed" (15–21 Apr) — almost certainly stale. Both freeboards at 1.50 m (Yellow); limited storage capacity.
- **Tails 1B safety-flag inconsistency — sixth day** — marked "Safe" on 16, 17, 18, 20, 21 Apr despite unchanged +4 m stack-point, +2.5 m base, +1.50 m berm-height variances. Contradicts 13–15 Apr "NOT safe" flags and asymmetric with BB2 (+1.60 m berm-height variance drew the Orange flag).
- **[[PDP/TSF Automation Project]]** — 12-action programme to centralise SCADA monitoring and automate pump control. 5 of 12 actions complete. Critical path: flow meter and TSF comms interface procurement (lead times to Jun 2026).
- **Float-off Pond** — WL 9.70 mRL on 21 Apr (+0.10 from stable 9.60). Rainfall erosion at entrance to be corrected; discharge to be extended.
- **DMA–SMO mining face seepage** — continuing 17, 18, 20, 21 Apr; raise the floor.
- **Geotech-report data-quality bugs** — TSF Non-Compliance row "None" contradicting narrative across 18, 20, 21 Apr. Old DMB Pit Non-Compliance row "None" on 21 Apr despite Red Alert. PDP-1/PDP-2 liner-theft paragraphs verbatim identical across four consecutive reports. Paddock 9C "WL to be confirmed" for seven consecutive days. No §4 weekly priority-actions table on 21 Apr. Finishing Pond omitted as standalone row on 21 Apr.
