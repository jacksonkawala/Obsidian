## Critical Observations

### Deposition Continues at Steady Pace — 7-Day Average Settling
Slimes received today: **50,313 m³** at 1.15 t/m³ density (≈ 12,040 t solids added). This follows the 67,492 m³ spike on 15 Apr. The deposition chart now shows three consecutive working days: ~2,000 dry t (14 Apr), ~16,000 dry t (15 Apr), ~12,000 dry t (16 Apr). The **7-day average pumped volume** has risen to 46,125 m³/day with a 7-day average solids intake of 4,355 t/day.

At the reported 95-day remaining capacity and the revised 1.2 Mt storage, the implied required production rate is ~5,170 t/day. The 7-day average (4,355 t/day) is below that, suggesting either the plant is not yet back to full throughput or the revised capacity will last longer than 95 days.

### Freeboard Slipping Toward 5.0 m (Orange Threshold)
Minimum freeboard dropped to **5.40 m** (from 5.50 m on 15 Apr and 5.70 m on 14 Apr). Pool rose +0.10 m day-on-day (24.50 → 24.60 mRL using the Op-Notes reading on both days — the Section 4 table on the 17 Apr geotech report confirms 24.60 mRL). Yellow TARP remains; the Orange threshold sits at **5.00 m** (0.40 m away). The pool is rising despite the small reduction in slimes volume today vs 15 Apr, indicating return pumping is not yet keeping pace with deposition.

### Water Return Slightly Softer
- Water returned TSF → Pond: **19,926.72 m³/d** — down from 20,219 on 15 Apr
- Water received at TSF: **50,313 m³** (matches slimes volume)
- All four pumps (Supernatant 1, Supernatant 2, EDP 1, EDP 2) remain at 100% availability — table still shows the duplicate "Supernatant Pump 1" label (see [[#Data Quality Notes]])
- Return ratio: 40% of daily inflow (improved from 30% on 15 Apr thanks to lower slimes volume, not higher return volume)

### Lift 1 Construction Still Stalled — Completion Slips Another Day
- Fill to date: 245,230 m³ (unchanged — zero new fill for fourth consecutive day)
- 7-day average fill: 0 m³
- Estimated completion slipped to **25 May 2026** (was 24 May on 15 Apr, 23 May on 14 Apr, 21 May on 13 Apr, 19 May on 12 Apr — a cumulative 6-day slip over five days of stall)
- ADTs loading sand to improve access; runoff siphoning between Valley 3 and TSF toe berms ongoing (agreed with EoR)
- TPH lab services on site; QC equipment procurement ongoing
- Eroded Lift 1 wall sections from recent rainfall require reinstatement

- Prolonged 3A stacker washouts have flooded the access road and eroded the compacted Lift 1 sidewall. The stacker was repositioned (done). Retention berms at the new position are under construction to contain temporary washouts and protect drains. Plant currently shut, so no new low-density discharge.
- The just-mentioned starter wall liner theft (see the 16 Apr geotech report) exposes wall sections to rainfall erosion. Reinstatement of liners on the affected section is urgently required.
