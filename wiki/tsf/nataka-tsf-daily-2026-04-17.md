## Critical Observations

### Pool Drops 0.5 m — Freeboard Back to 5.90 m (Yellow, Comfortably Above Orange)

The most significant change from the 16 Apr report is a **0.50 m drawdown in pool elevation** (24.60 → 24.10 mRL), lifting minimum freeboard back to **5.90 m**. That restores a 0.90 m cushion above the 5.00 m Orange threshold, up from 0.40 m a day earlier. Four return-water pumps are deployed and commissioned, and the plant is on shutdown — so there is no new low-density slurry discharge to offset the drawdown.

### Slurry Receipt Much Lower Today

Slimes received today: **15,884 m³** at 1.22 t/m³ density (≈ 5,575 t solids added). That is well below the 67,492 m³ spike on 15 Apr and the 50,313 m³ receipt on 16 Apr, reflecting the plant shutdown. The **7-day average** is essentially unchanged at 46,126 m³/day (5,121 t/day solids) — the surge days keep the rolling average up even as today's flow drops.

### Water Return Surges as Pool is Drawn Down

- Water returned TSF → Pond: **34,394 m³/d** — up sharply from 19,927 m³/d on 16 Apr, reflecting the commissioning of the fourth return-water pump and active drawdown of the pool
- Return now **exceeds** today's slurry receipt by a factor of 2.2, which is what the 0.5 m pool drop is telling us
- All four pumps (Supernatant 1, Supernatant 2, EDP 1, EDP 2) report 100% availability — though the **"Supernatant Pump 1" label still appears twice** in the table (persistent copy-paste issue; see [[#Data Quality Notes]])

### Lift 1 Still Stalled — Completion Slips to 26 May

- Fill to date: 245,230 m³ (unchanged — zero new fill for the fifth consecutive day)
- 7-day average fill: 0 m³
- Estimated completion slipped to **26 May 2026** (was 25 May on 16 Apr — a further 1-day slip, and now a 7-day cumulative slip from the 19 May estimate on 12 Apr)
- ADTs continue loading sand to improve access; runoff siphoning between Valley 3 and TSF toe berms ongoing (agreed with EoR)
- TPH lab services on site; QC equipment procurement ongoing
- Erosion on Lift 1 wall from earlier rainfall still requires reinstatement
- Margin to Phase 1 capacity: **55 days** (26 May → 20 Jul)

### 3A Stacker Stopped and Relocated; Plant on Shutdown

The 3A stacker has been formally **stopped and relocated**, following repeated washouts that encroached on Lift 1 and eroded the compacted sidewall. Retention berms at the new position are still under construction. With the plant on shut, no new low-density discharge is contributing to the washout cycle.

### Starter-Wall Liner Theft — Rehabilitation Flagged

Rainfall-induced erosion continues on the exposed section of the TSF starter wall where liner was previously removed. Urgent rehabilitation and liner reinstatement are now formally flagged in red in the report body.

## Data Quality Notes

1. **Pool-elevation mismatch inside the report.** Section 1 summary table still reads "pool elevation @ 24.6 mRL" and "minimum weekly freeboard 5.4 m" — both are 16 Apr values that were not refreshed when §2 Op Notes and §4 Pool table were updated to 24.10 mRL / 5.90 m. Structural issue that makes the headline figures misleading if read in isolation.
2. **§4 header dated "18/04/2026"** while the report date is 17-04-2026 — a one-day-forward template error, repeated pattern across several recent reports.
3. **Cover-page aerial dated "18-03-2026"** — one month stale. Likely a template image that was not rotated.
4. **"Supernatant Pump 1" listed twice** in the Return Water table — same bug as 15 and 16 Apr; the second row almost certainly refers to Supernatant Pump 2.
5. **Freeboard "previous day = 5.40 m"** reconciles with the 16 Apr summary (freeboard 5.40 m, pool 24.60 mRL), so the report is internally consistent on the previous-day fields even where the current-day header row is stale.
6. **"Total Solids to TSF = 266,559 m³"** in the §3 table is anomalous for a daily figure — likely a mis-labelled cumulative volume, not today's receipt.
7. **PDP balance −17,156 m³/d with zero reported inflow.** Either unmetered supply (e.g., raw water or a misallocated inflow) is feeding PDP, or the pond is actively being drawn down. Worth reconciling with the PDP level at morning inspection (geotech 18 Apr shows PDP-1 WL unchanged at 34.00 mRL — so if PDP is stable, an unmetered inflow is the more plausible explanation).
