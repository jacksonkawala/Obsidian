## Critical Observations

### Pool Back Up to 24.75 mRL — Freeboard Drops Through 5.25 m

After the 0.50 m drawdown on 17 Apr that pulled the pool to 24.10 mRL and lifted freeboard to 5.90 m, the pool has **rebounded 0.65 m over two days** to 24.75 mRL. Minimum freeboard is now **5.25 m (Yellow)** — only **0.25 m above the 5.00 m Orange threshold**, and the closest the TSF has sat to Orange since the current event sequence began. The trend visible on the QPO chart is a clear downward drift from ~6.6 m in late March.

### Lift 1 Construction Has Resumed

The five-day stall broke during the 19 Apr shift: **600 m³ of fill placed overnight** — the first non-zero fill since 12 Apr. The 7-day average ticks up to 86 m³/day. Fill to date rises to 245,830 m³. Estimated completion slips to **28 May 2026** (was 26 May on 17 Apr) — cumulatively 9 days of slip since the 19 May estimate on 12 Apr. Margin to Phase 1 capacity: 53 days.

### Slurry Receipt Recovers to Mid-Range

Slimes received today: **53,921 m³** at 1.13 t/m³ density (≈ 11,182 t solids). That is well above the 15,884 m³ receipt of 17 Apr and slightly above the 50,313 m³ receipt of 16 Apr — the plant is back online and running at elevated cadence. The 7-day average pumped volume is essentially flat at 46,128 m³/day; solids average 6,718 t/day.

- Water returned TSF → Pond: **16,534.56 m³/d** — down sharply from 34,394 m³/d on 17 Apr (when pumps were actively drawing the pool down) and also below the 19,927 m³/d on 16 Apr
- Return ratio: 31% of today's receipt (down from 40% on 16 Apr, and well below 17 Apr's 217% draw-down regime)
- All four pumps report 100% availability — "Supernatant Pump 1" **still listed twice**; see [[#Data Quality Notes]]
