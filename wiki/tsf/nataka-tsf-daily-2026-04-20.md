## Data Quality Notes

1. **Three pool/beach elevation values inside the report.** §4 table: 24.70 mRL. Summary-table freeboard footnote: 24.75 mRL. Summary-table slurry-volume footnote: beach 24.80 mRL. Same pattern as 15 Apr's three-way inconsistency — structural issue that recurs.
2. **§4 header dated "21/04/2026"** while report date is 20-04-2026 — one-day-forward template error persists for the fourth consecutive edition.
3. **Cover-page aerial dated "18-03-2026"** — one month stale.
4. **"Supernatant Pump 1" listed twice** — sixth consecutive edition with this bug.
5. **"Total Solids to TSF = 271,873 m³"** in §3 — cumulative-period figure mislabelled as today's value (same pattern as 17 and 19 Apr).
6. **Lift 1 fill to date = 245,830 m³** on both 19 and 20 Apr despite a stated 1,370 m³ fill on 20 Apr. Either the fill-to-date field was not refreshed, or today's fill was not yet rolled in when the report was produced. Worth reconciling.
7. **PDP balance −14,974 m³/d with zero inflows** — mass-balance gap unchanged from 17 and 19 Apr. The 20 Apr geotech confirms PDP-1 and PDP-2 WLs unchanged (34.00 and 31.90 mRL), so an unmetered inflow must be present.
8. **WCPA balance +44,927 m³/d** — large positive imbalance. Inflows ~69,693 vs outflows ~24,767 doesn't reconcile with the stated balance; either the balance cell or one of the flow cells is in error.
9. **Tails outflow reported as 20,979 m³/d** today vs 66,865 on 16, 17, 19 Apr — a 3× decrease that is not narrated. Plausibly reflects the plant running at reduced throughput, but would expect narrative support.
10. **Pool went 24.10 (18 Apr geotech) → ~24.30 (19 Apr TSF prev-day implied) → 24.75 (19 Apr) → 24.75 (20 Apr summary footnote) → 24.70 (20 Apr §4).** Trajectory recoverable only by reading the geotech, the 19 Apr TSF summary, and the 20 Apr summary together — no single source shows the full sequence.
