# Nataka TSF Daily Summary Report — 21 April 2026

**Ref:** 53/TSF/2026 | **Date:** 21-04-2026 | **Cover image date:** 18-03-2026

## Highlights

### TSF Pool Draws Down Slightly; Freeboard Recovers to 5.40 m

Pool level is reported as **24.60 mRL** in both Operational Notes and the §4 table. Minimum freeboard improves from 5.30 m to **5.40 m (Yellow)**, now 0.40 m above the 5.00 m Orange threshold. Four pumps are still reported deployed and commissioned.

### Lift 1 Construction Continues, but 3A Stacker Damage Becomes the Main Risk

Lift 1 remains **76% complete**, with **960 m³** placed across day and night shift. The report repeats the 3A stacker washout narrative: wet tailings have encroached on Lift 1, eroded the sidewall, flooded access, and created HME bogging risk in liquefied sand tailings. The stacker relocation is marked done; impacted sections are being removed, recompacted, and retested.

### Water Return Exceeds Daily Slimes Receipt

Slimes received at TSF: **16,175 m³**. Water returned TSF → Pond: **21,742 m³**, giving a return ratio of about 134% and aligning with the slight freeboard recovery. All return pumps are again shown at 100% availability, but the table still lists "Supernatant Pump 1" twice.
