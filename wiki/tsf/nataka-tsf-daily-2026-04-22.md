# Nataka TSF Daily Summary Report — 22 April 2026

**Ref:** 53/TSF/2026 | **Date:** 22-04-2026 | **Cover image date:** 18-03-2026

## Highlights

### TSF Reassessed to 62% Filled

The filled fraction steps from 61% to **62%** under the revised production/dry-density model. Total solids received to date rise to **748,156 t**, leaving the unverified 1.2 Mt capacity model as the controlling planning assumption. Bathymetric survey remains required because pool-volume monitoring is still suspended.

### Pool/Freeboard Stable, with a Small Internal Mismatch

Operational Notes report pool level **24.55 mRL**, while the §4 table reports **24.60 mRL** and **5.40 m** freeboard. Freeboard is unchanged from 21 Apr and remains Yellow, 0.40 m above the Orange threshold.

### High Slimes Day, High Return Day

Daily slimes receipt rises to **52,056 m³** at 1.17 t/m³. Water returned TSF → Pond is **41,900 m³**, about 80% of daily receipt. This is a much more active hydraulic day than 21 Apr, but freeboard remains flat.

### Lift 1 Fill Improves, While 3A Damage Remains Open

Lift 1 construction remains 76% complete but placed **1,510 m³** across day and night shift. The 3A stacker washout narrative remains active: wet tailings have reached and eroded the Lift 1 compacted wall area, with access, stability, and HME immobilisation risks.
