# Nataka TSF Daily Summary Report — 23 April 2026

**Ref:** 54/TSF/2026 | **Date:** 23-04-2026 | **Cover image date:** 18-03-2026

## Highlights

### TSF Reassessed to 63% Filled

The filled fraction steps again to **63%**. Total solids received to date are **751,358 t**, a +3,202 t increase from 22 Apr despite one table still carrying a very large slimes-volume number. This reinforces that the percentage changes are being driven by updated production/dry-density forecasting as well as daily solids.

### Freeboard Drops Back to 5.30 m

Pool elevation rises to **24.70 mRL** in both Operational Notes and §4. Minimum freeboard drops from 5.40 m to **5.30 m (Yellow)**, again only 0.30 m above the 5.00 m Orange threshold.

### Return-Water Numbers Conflict with Slimes Table

The slimes table reports **52,056 m³** received today, but the return-water table reports "Water received at TSF" as only **8,030 m³** and water returned TSF → Pond as **6,444 m³**. Those two fields cannot both describe the same daily TSF inflow; this is the main audit issue in the 23 Apr report.

### Lift 1 Still Moving, but 3A Washout Narrative Unchanged

Lift 1 remains **76% complete** with **780 m³** placed on day and night shift. The report repeats the same 3A stacker/Lift 1 risk text: wet tailings encroaching on the Lift 1 wall, access flooding, liquefied-tailings HME bogging risk, recompaction and retesting in progress.
