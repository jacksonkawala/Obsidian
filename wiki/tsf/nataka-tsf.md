# Nataka TSF

Tailings Storage Facility at [[Kenmare Moma Mining (Mauritius) Limited]]'s Moma Mine in Mozambique. The facility stores mineral processing tailings (slimes) from heavy mineral sands operations.

## Latest Update (as of 24 Apr 2026)

- **TSF filled:** 63% by the 23 Apr TSF summary, with **751,358 t** solids received to date under the revised/unverified 1.2 Mt capacity model.
- **Pool/freeboard:** 24.70 mRL and **5.30 m freeboard (Yellow)** in the 24 Apr geotech report, back to only **0.30 m above the Orange threshold** after a brief 5.40-5.45 m recovery on 21-23 Apr.
- **Lift 1 construction:** still **76% complete** in the 21-23 Apr TSF summaries. Daily fill: 960 m³ (21 Apr), 1,510 m³ (22 Apr), 780 m³ (23 Apr). The 3A stacker washout/overtopping remains the active construction threat.
- **Tails 3A:** still overtopping the retention berm on 22, 23, and 24 Apr; wet tailings have encroached on and eroded Lift 1 sidewall, with HME bogging, dissipator blockage, and delay risks.
- **WCPC:** Water Body remains **Orange but critical** at 1.40 m freeboard. WCP-C Dredge Pond was deliberately raised to **10.50 mRL** on 24 Apr to refloat a beached plant; the plant is not operational and the report says water must not exceed 10.50 mRL.
- **Old DMB Pit:** Red Alert persisted 21-23 Apr at 21.50 mRL / 1.50 m freeboard, improving slightly on 24 Apr to 21.35 mRL / 1.65 m but still called Red.
- **WCPB HTC / Lake Johari risk:** new 22-24 Apr thread; HTC discharge on WCPB starboard side is reducing tailings-to-sidewall freeboard and creating backflow risk toward Lake Johari.

## Status Snapshot (through 21 Apr 2026)

- **Phase 1** capacity: 741,000 t (original design volume)
- **Revised capacity (unverified):** 1,200,000 t — based on assumed dry density and calibrated models; bathymetric survey required to confirm
- **Solids stored to date:** 728,620 t (+3,060 t on 20 Apr; +19,817 t over the 16–20 Apr window) — **61% filled** (revised model; rebased from 59% → 60% → 61% on successive reports 16 → 19 → 20 Apr)
- **Remaining capacity:** 471,380 t / 91 days (revised, per 20 Apr report)
- **Phase 1 capacity date:** 20 July 2026 (revised, unchanged through the series)
- **Pool elevation:** 24.70 mRL (21 Apr geotech morning, §4 table; but 21 Apr Highlights narrative reads "currently at 24.75 mRL" — a new intra-report contradiction). Trajectory: 24.10 (17/18 Apr after drawdown) → ~24.30 → 24.75 (19 Apr) → 24.70 (20 Apr) → 24.70 (21 Apr).
- **[[Freeboard]]:** 5.30 m (YELLOW TARP) — **0.30 m from the 5.00 m Orange threshold**. Oscillating 5.2–6.65 m since early April with a clear downward drift.
- **Lift 1 construction has resumed** after a five-day stall (12–17 Apr). Overnight fill of 600 m³ on 19 Apr; day+night shift fill of 1,370 m³ on 20 Apr. 7-day average 281 m³/day.
- **Slurry receipt mixed** — 50,313 m³ (16 Apr), 15,884 m³ (17 Apr, plant shut), 53,921 m³ (19 Apr, plant restarted), 13,702 m³ (20 Apr). Week-17 7-day average 46,129 m³/day, 7,155 t/day solids.

## Lift 1 Construction

The Lift 1 raise is underway to extend TSF capacity beyond Phase 1:

- **Fill target:** 325,000 m³
- **Fill to date:** 245,830 m³ (76% complete — stepped up from 245,230 after the 19 Apr 600 m³ overnight shift)
- **Remaining:** ~79,170 m³
- **Est. completion:** 29 May 2026 (slipped from 28 May on 19 Apr, 26 May on 17 Apr, 25 May on 16 Apr, 19 May on 12 Apr — **10-day cumulative slip** over eight days)
- **Current working level:** 33 RL (south side, chainage 0+100 to 0+175)
- **Method:** 750 mm sand layers, compacted (8+ roller passes), verified by DCP and Troxler tests

**Construction resumed on 19 Apr** after a five-day stall (13–17 Apr). 600 m³ placed overnight on 19 Apr; 1,370 m³ (day + night shift) on 20 Apr; 7-day average 281 m³/day. The Tails 3A stacker was formally stopped and relocated on 17 Apr; plant remained on shut through the 19–20 Apr window to allow density control and retention-berm construction. On 20 Apr the retention berm at the relocated 3A position was reported **overtopped** — an escalation beyond the "narrow and short" concern flagged on 17 Apr. Capacity margin is now **52 days** under the revised model (29 May completion vs 20 Jul capacity). TPH lab services on site. QC equipment for wall lift construction identified and procurement ongoing. See [[Lift 1 Construction Log]], [[Nataka TSF Daily Summary Report — 20 April 2026]], [[Nataka TSF Daily Summary Report — 19 April 2026]], and [[Daily Geotechnical Report — 20 April 2026]].

## Infrastructure

- **Supernatant pumps:** 2x — both at 100% availability as of 09 Apr (restored from priming faults). When running, Supernatant 1 delivers ~1,500 m³/hr.
- **Slimes extraction pumps (EDPs):** 4x (portside PS6030, starboard PS6031, centre PS0543, 4th pump). EDP 1 and EDP 2 at 100% availability as of 09 Apr (restored from high-WL shutdown). Typical flow 400–550 m³/hr each at 1.12–1.28 SGU.
- **Electrical pumps:** 2x (previously both down — V-belt failures)
- **Decanting pumps:** 2x — access to Pump No. 2 being created (red sand haulage for access road)
- **Talweg pump:** Routes water to PDP; stopped when PDP WL is high
- **Flocculant plant:** Down (Main Plant A shutdown; chronic silo issues)
- **PDP return water pumps:** 4x (6040–6043) — manage pond water level at ~31.7 mRL
- **Groundwater drains (GWD):** Currently inaccessible (talweg safety)
- **Sand drains:** Currently inaccessible (talweg safety)

See [[TSF Operations Log]] for detailed pump status history.

## Key Concerns

1. **Capacity model revised but unverified** — the 14 Apr TSF report raised available capacity from 741,000 t to 1,200,000 t, extending the deadline from 05 Jun to 20 Jul 2026. However, this is based on assumed dry density and calibrated models; a bathymetric survey is urgently required to confirm. Under the original model, only ~12 days remain (vs 91 under the revised).
2. **Freeboard drifting toward Orange** — the trend since early April is clearly downward: 6.65 m (12 Apr) → 5.65/5.70/5.50/5.40 (13–16 Apr) → 5.90 (17 Apr drawdown) → 5.70 (implied 18 Apr) → 5.25 (19 Apr) → 5.30 (20 Apr). Yellow TARP; **0.30 m from the 5.00 m Orange threshold** on 20 Apr.
3. **Bathymetric survey overdue** — pool volume monitoring suspended; QPO review blocked
4. **Liner integrity** — theft continues relentlessly. Fresh starter-wall liner thefts recorded on 17, 18, 20 Apr; PDP-1 and PDP-2 port-sidewall liners repeatedly ripped off. Exposed sections eroding during rainy season (though Weeks 16 and 17 both recorded zero rainfall).
5. **Seepage at Lift 2 toe** — observed since 08 Apr, still reported through 20 Apr; side-berm seepage flagged explicitly on 18 and 20 Apr.
6. **Pipeline hanging** — slimes pipeline on starter-wall bend hanging into TSF, progressive collapse risk; repositioning and securing recommended (unchanged 17–20 Apr)
7. **Drainage access** — GWD and sand drains inaccessible due to talweg conditions; both WTD trend charts remain blank
8. **Water-balance sign flips daily** — TSF balance +26,499 m³/d (16 Apr accumulating) → −22,398 (17 Apr drawdown) → +33,498 (19 Apr after pool refill) → −7,038 (20 Apr neutral). PDP balance shows a persistent **zero-inflow anomaly** on 17, 19, and 20 Apr (with large negative balances that don't match observed stable PDP-1/PDP-2 water levels — likely unmetered raw-water supply).
9. **Lift 1 roller bottleneck** — KME2509 on long-term breakdown, C030 turbo failure from late Mar. Resumption on 19 Apr brought the 7-day average to 281 m³/d — still very low vs design.
10. **Active stacker concerns** — **Tails 3A retention berm overtopped on 20 Apr and still overtopping on 21 Apr** (unresolved). Plant on shut. BB2 berm-height variance +1.60 m Orange flag unchanged 17, 18, 20, 21 Apr. BB1 return-water migration concern of 17 Apr now embedded in BB1 / BB1_Kwale stacker remarks ("construct retention berm to prevent water/slurry migration towards DMC pit") rather than a standalone narrative — still unresolved.
11. **WCPC downgraded to Orange on 21 Apr** — finally! Trajectory: 1.05 (17 Apr) → 1.05 (18 Apr) → 1.20 (20 Apr) → **1.40 m Orange (21 Apr)**. Still non-compliant and narrative still calls condition "critical." Godwin pump running; HMC stacker **still listed as inflow across four consecutive reports** despite recommendation to halt all inflows.
12. **MSP Rejects cascade — now Old DM Pit RED Alert (21 Apr).** Old DM Pit Orange 15 Apr → redirect to P16C → P16C backflow 16–18 Apr → Old DM Pit Orange again 20 Apr → **Old DM Pit RED on 21 Apr** (+0.35 m to 21.50 mRL, FB 1.50 m). MSP Rejects now rerouted back to P16C; inflows to Old DM Pit ceased. Paddock 11C separation-berm erosion persists 17–21 Apr. Non-Compliance row on 21 Apr reads "None" despite the Red Alert — contradicts narrative.
13. **Paddock 73 — Orange Alert 18 Apr, since recovered** — +0.25 m on 18 Apr (41.60 → 41.85 mRL, Berm 73 FB 1.85 m Orange) triggered Orange Alert. Dewatering (Godwin + Hippo to PDP-1) dropped WL to 40.90 mRL by 20 Apr, freeboard back to 2.80 m. Seepage at Berm 73 toe continues under monitoring.
14. **SMO separation-berm breach risk** — escalated 17 Apr, **sliding further 18 Apr** (front-berm FB 0.90 m — below 1.0 m threshold). Recovered to 1.20 m by 20 Apr but still flagged. S1 stacker to be retained with new retention berm (20 Apr wording); S2 to be lifted.
15. **WCPB Starboardside Corner Red Alert** — WL unchanged at 14.50 mRL across 17, 18, 20, 21 Apr (five readings). River-sidewall freeboard 1.50 m, dewatering in progress; plans under consideration to reshape the corner.
16. **Tails 1B safety-flag inconsistency — now sixth day** (16, 17, 18, 20, 21 Apr — no 19 Apr geotech). Marked "Safe to Operate = Yes" despite unchanged +4 m stack-point, +2.5 m base, and +1.50 m berm-height variances. Worth escalating with the inspection team; asymmetric with BB2, where +1.60 m berm-height variance drew the Orange flag.
17. **WCPC Concentrator beaching risk (new 18 Apr)** — oversize-material island behind the plant; recommendation to move concentrator forward. C1 tails O/E "Safe to Operate = No" first flagged 18 Apr, unchanged 20 Apr.
18. **HTC backflow risk toward Mualadi Lake (18 Apr)** — buildup exceeds the 1.0 m freeboard from beach to NGL.
19. **Persistent TSF-summary data-quality bugs** (15–20 Apr editions): summary-table pool/freeboard row not refreshed to match §4 Pool table and Op Notes (typical 0.10–0.65 m mismatch within the same report); "Supernatant Pump 1" listed twice in Return Water table; §4 table header dated one day ahead of report date; cover-page aerial dated 18-03-2026 (one month stale). Six consecutive editions with these unresolved.

## Water Balance (WCPA System, 20 Apr)

The TSF sits within the broader WCPA (Water Containment and Processing Area) system:

- **TSF daily balance:** −7,038 m³/d (inflow 13,702 from PDP; outflows 16,852 to pond + 3,888 seepage/evap) — mildly negative, matches the 0.05 m pool drop
- **PDP daily balance:** −14,974 m³/d (zero reported inflow; outflows 13,702 to TSF + 1,272 seepage). **Mass-balance gap**: PDP-1 and PDP-2 WLs have been stable for days, so unmetered inflow (likely raw water) is present but not captured in the table. Same anomaly on 17 and 19 Apr.
- **WCPA daily balance:** +44,927 m³/d (inflows from TSF return 16,852 + RAW Dam 16,344 + Makeup 19,714 + Stacker R/W 16,783; outflows Cons 620 + Tails 20,979 + Seepage 3,168). The Tails outflow on 20 Apr (20,979 m³/d) is ~3× lower than the 66,865 m³/d reported 16–19 Apr — a step-change that is not narrated and is worth reconciling.
